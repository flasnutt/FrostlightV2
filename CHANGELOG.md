# Changelog

## 1.6.0
- Removed Spotify's promo / announcement box on Home.
- Home banner reworked: title always on top and readable, candy-cane frame, drifting northern lights and twinkling stars.
- New: snowy pine forest in the corner of playlist headers, snow bank at the bottom of the library.

## 1.5.0
- Fixed: hard seam under the playlist header and the abrupt end of the gradient; it is now continuous and fades out.
- Fixed: play buttons are round again; green accents (playing speaker icon) instead of red.
- New: every song row gets a winter icon (tree, snowflake, candy cane, gift, bauble, holly),
  Christmas lettering, a red hover and a green glow on the playing song.
- New: gift bow on the playlist cover.

## 1.4.0
- Icicles reworked: two layers of different lengths with glints and a frosty glaze along the top edge.
- Candy-cane underline beneath section titles.

## 1.3.0
- Volume slider is now only a string of lights: no bar, the played lights glow and the rest are dimmed.
- New: icicles along the top of the window, Santa hat on the cover art, snowflake cursor,
  red/green context menu and glowing sidebar icons.

## 1.2.1
- Removed yellow from the UI: buttons, equalizer and accents are now red, green and white.
- Play buttons are red baubles everywhere; playlist headers use a red/blue/green gradient.
- Chips, hovered rows and the playing track are recoloured; song and card titles use the Christmas font.

## 1.2.0
- Fixed: the song progress bar and the volume bar no longer share styling.
- Song progress bar is now a pure candy cane that glides smoothly instead of jumping.
- Volume bar is now a string of Christmas lights; the filled part is lit, the rest is dim.
- New: Christmas font (Mountains of Christmas) for titles.
- New: candy-cane scrollbar, holly on cards, pine garland on the player bar,
  red bauble play button, gold stars beside section titles.

## 1.1.0
- Fixed: the Home banner no longer overlaps the shortcut tiles.
- New: candy-cane progress bar with a chasing string of Christmas lights on the unplayed part.
- New: repeat and shuffle turn holly green when active.

## 1.0.0
- First release.
- Animated snowfall, cabin banner on the Home page, snow-capped cards, Playfair Display titles.
- Colour schemes: `frost` and `ember`.
